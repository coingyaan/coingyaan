How to read the Bitcoin Market Outlook

The Bitcoin Market Outlook answers one question. Based on current market data what is the most probable direction of Bitcoin over the next 24 hours. It is a probability estimate built from live signals, not a call on where price will be at any exact moment. A reading of 58 percent upside means the balance of signals leans higher. It does not mean Bitcoin cannot fall.

What the outlook gives you

The expected 24 hour direction, an upside and downside probability, the expected trading range, the current market condition, a confidence level and the strongest signals shaping today's view. Direction tells you the lean. Confidence tells you how much the signals agree. A strong lean with low confidence means the signals point one way but do not agree, so the edge is thin.

How CoinGyaan calculates it

We pull free public market data from major exchanges then run it through our own engine. Trend comes from moving average structure. Momentum comes from RSI and MACD. Funding and open interest describe how leveraged traders are positioned. Volatility sets the expected range and shapes risk. Sentiment adds context. Each signal is scored and weighted, and the weights are transparent and shown above. The data providers can change over time but the interpretation is ours.

Why confidence matters as much as direction

A direction on its own can mislead. Confidence is earned here, not typed. It rises when the signals agree and are strong, and it falls when volatility is high or the signals conflict. That is why the outlook can read bullish with low confidence on a choppy day. Reading both together is the point.

Frequently asked questions

Is this financial advice?

No. The Bitcoin Market Outlook is market analysis. It helps you assess the most probable short term direction. Some people may use it alongside prediction markets such as Polymarket or Kalshi but it remains analysis and not betting advice or financial advice. Always do your own research.

How often does it update?

The outlook refreshes every 5 minutes from live market data. The last updated time is shown at the top of the panel so you always know how fresh the reading is.

What data does it use?

Version 1 uses free public data including price, candles, volume, funding and open interest from major exchanges plus market dominance and a sentiment index. The architecture is built so premium data can be added later without changing what you see.
