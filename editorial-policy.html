Editorial Policy

How we research, write, review and update content on CoinGyaan.

Summary: All content on CoinGyaan is created and reviewed by Monesh Kumar, a crypto builder and Web3 entrepreneur with over seven years of experience in the cryptocurrency industry since 2017. We do not publish financial advice. All content is educational and informational only.

Who Creates Our Content

CoinGyaan is founded and operated by Monesh Kumar, based in Bangalore, India. Monesh has been active in the cryptocurrency space since 2017 covering two complete market cycles including the 2017 to 2018 bull run, the 2020 to 2021 DeFi summer and subsequent bear markets.

All guides, analysis pieces and tool explanations published on CoinGyaan are either written directly by Monesh Kumar or reviewed and approved by him before publication. We do not publish anonymous content or content from unverified sources without editorial review.

Monesh's background includes building on-chain tools, participating in blockchain hackathons, managing a crypto community of over 1,000 members on Telegram since 2017, running a YouTube channel covering crypto education and building Web3 tools including the CoinGyaan Sentiment Checker and Airdrop Rescue Tool.

Our Research Standards

Every guide published on CoinGyaan is researched using a combination of the following sources:

  • On-chain data from publicly available blockchain explorers
  • Market data from CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap and Binance public APIs
  • Official project documentation and whitepapers
  • Reputable industry publications including CoinDesk and CoinTelegraph
  • Direct experience participating in DeFi protocols, airdrops and Web3 ecosystems

We do not rely on single sources for factual claims. Where data is time-sensitive or subject to change we include the date of the information and recommend readers verify current figures independently.

Editorial Independence

CoinGyaan maintains full editorial independence. Sponsored content or advertising placements do not influence our editorial judgments or guide content. If a piece of content is sponsored or promotional it is clearly labeled as such.

We do not accept payment to review projects positively or to suppress negative information about any crypto protocol or company. All opinions expressed on CoinGyaan represent our honest assessment based on publicly available information and direct experience.

Accuracy and Corrections

We take accuracy seriously. The cryptocurrency market moves quickly and information can become outdated. We follow these practices to maintain accuracy:

  • All guides include a publication date and are reviewed for accuracy at the time of writing
  • Tool data is pulled in real time from public APIs and reflects live market conditions
  • When errors are identified we correct them promptly and note the correction where significant
  • Guides covering rapidly changing topics such as active airdrop campaigns are reviewed and updated when major changes occur

If you identify an error or outdated information in any CoinGyaan content please contact us at hello@coingyaan.com and we will investigate and correct it promptly.

No Financial Advice

CoinGyaan does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations or trading signals. All content including our sentiment tools, Fear and Greed Index, guides and market analysis is for educational and informational purposes only.

Cryptocurrency investments carry significant risk including the potential loss of all invested capital. Past market conditions shown on our tools do not predict future outcomes. Always conduct your own research and consult a qualified financial advisor before making any investment decisions.

Our full risk disclosure is available in our Disclaimer.

Transparency on Tools and Data

CoinGyaan's sentiment tools and indices use publicly available data from the following sources:

  • Alternative.me Fear and Greed Index API for the Fear and Greed score
  • Binance public API for RSI, moving averages and volume data
  • CoinGecko public API for price data, market cap and dominance metrics

We are transparent about how our Market Condition Score is calculated. The score is a weighted combination of RSI, 200-day moving average, volume surge, Fear and Greed and institutional momentum signals. It is an educational indicator not a trading signal.

Conflict of Interest Policy

Where CoinGyaan has a direct relationship with a project we write about, such as participating in their points campaign or holding their tokens, we disclose this within the relevant content. Our Concrete review, for example, includes our invite code and participation in their campaign which is clearly stated in the article.

We believe transparency about participation is better than exclusion. Our analysis remains based on publicly verifiable facts regardless of any community involvement.
