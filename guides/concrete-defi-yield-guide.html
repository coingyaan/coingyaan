What Is Concrete

Concrete is a full-stack yield infrastructure protocol built for DeFi. Its core product is institutional-grade ERC-4626 vaults that automatically allocate, rebalance and compound yield across the best on-chain opportunities. Instead of you manually moving funds between lending markets, liquidity pools and yield strategies, Concrete handles all of it in one click.

The key difference from most DeFi yield products is that Concrete is built from the ground up for institutional standards. That means automated accounting with daily NAV precision, real-time security monitoring via Hypernative, 47 completed audits and a $250,000 USDC bug bounty with Cantina. This is not a yield farm with a flashy UI. It is infrastructure designed to handle serious capital.

Concrete currently operates across Ethereum, Berachain, Arbitrum, Morph and Katana with more chains being added. Its flagship product, the Concrete DeFi USDT vault, targets approximately 8.5% stable yield through delta-neutral arbitrage strategies across perpetuals and lending markets.

How Concrete Vaults Work

Concrete vaults use the ERC-4626 standard which is the most widely adopted tokenized vault standard in DeFi. When you deposit into a Concrete vault you receive ct[asset] tokens. These are yield-bearing vault shares that represent your position and can be used across other DeFi protocols adding composability to your yield.

Behind that simple deposit is a sophisticated system. The Automation Engine continuously accrues yield, updates vault accounting, calculates fees and maintains precise daily NAV. The Hook Manager enforces pre and post-transaction guardrails to keep capital within defined risk parameters. The Allocator manages capital deployment at market speed without governance delays.

This architecture separates Concrete from traditional DeFi vaults which often consolidate everything into a single multisig that controls strategy approval, execution and risk simultaneously. Concrete separates these roles by design, eliminating the single points of failure that caused over $3.1 billion in losses across Web3 in the first half of 2025.

Concrete vs Other DeFi Yield Protocols

Compared to other yield automation protocols, Concrete's institutional focus is its clearest differentiator. Protocols like Yearn Finance pioneered automated yield but were built for retail users in an earlier era of DeFi. Key Risks to Understand

Concrete is a serious protocol but DeFi risks always apply. Smart contract risk exists even with 47 audits. Yield is not guaranteed and can change based on market conditions. The Bags campaign does not guarantee any future token allocation. Cross-chain operations introduce bridge and interoperability risks.

Always use funds you can afford to lose when participating in any DeFi protocol. The Social Points Campaign activities that do not require deposits carry essentially no financial risk but any vault deposits involve the full spectrum of DeFi risks.
