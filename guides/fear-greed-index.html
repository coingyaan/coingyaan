How to Read the Crypto Fear and Greed Index

4 min read · CoinGyaan Team · February 2026

The Fear and Greed Index is one of the most referenced indicators in crypto. You see it everywhere. But most people just look at the number and move on without actually understanding what it means or how to use it.

Here is a simple breakdown.

What is the Fear and Greed Index?

It is a score from 0 to 100 that measures the overall emotional state of the crypto market. It combines multiple data signals into one number so you can quickly gauge whether the market is driven by fear or greed at any given moment.

What the Score Means

Score Zone What It Means
0 – 24 Extreme Fear Market is panicking. Historically a potential buying zone.
25 – 49 Fear Investors are cautious and nervous. Sentiment is weak.
50 – 74 Greed Confidence is building. Momentum is positive.
75 – 100 Extreme Greed Market is overheating. Higher risk of a correction.

What Signals Go Into It?

The index pulls from several sources including price volatility, market momentum, social media volume, Bitcoin dominance and search trends. No single signal controls the number. It is a combined picture of market emotion.

How Traders Actually Use It

The classic approach is contrarian. When everyone is in extreme fear, experienced traders often see it as a signal to look for buying opportunities. When everyone is in extreme greed, it is often a signal to be more cautious and consider taking some profits.

The famous Warren Buffett quote applies here perfectly: be fearful when others are greedy and greedy when others are fearful.

What It Does Not Tell You

The Fear and Greed Index does not predict price. It does not tell you when to buy or sell. It is one signal among many. A market can stay in extreme greed for weeks during a bull run. A market can sit in extreme fear for months during a bear market.

Use it as context and not as a trigger. Combine it with your own research and the other tools on CoinGyaan.
