Top AI Prompts for Crypto Research

5 min read · CoinGyaan Team · February 2026

Most traders waste hours scrolling through news sites, Twitter threads and YouTube videos before making a decision. A lot of noise and very little signal.

AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude can cut through that noise if you know how to ask the right questions. The prompt is everything. A bad prompt gives you a generic answer. A good prompt gives you a research analyst in your pocket.

Here are the prompts we actually use for crypto research.

1. Understand a Coin Quickly

Use this when you hear about a new project and want a fast but thorough overview without reading ten different articles.

Prompt
Explain [coin name] to me like I am a smart but busy investor. Cover what it does, who the team is, what problem it solves, who the competitors are and what the biggest risks are. Keep it under 300 words.

2. Analyze Market Sentiment

Use this when you want to understand the current community mood around a project before buying or selling.

Prompt
Based on what you know about [coin name], what is the general market sentiment right now? Is the community bullish or bearish? What are the main narratives driving price? What should I watch out for?

3. Evaluate a Token Before Buying

Use this as a quick due diligence checklist before committing any capital to a new position.

Prompt
I am considering buying [coin name]. Give me a quick due diligence checklist. Cover tokenomics, team credibility, use case strength, on-chain activity signals and any red flags I should research further.

4. Understand a Crypto Concept

Use this when you keep seeing a term like liquid staking or restaking or RWA and want to actually understand what it means.

Prompt
Explain [concept] in plain English. Give me a real world analogy, explain how it works in crypto, name 3 leading projects in this space and tell me the main risks involved.

5. Build a Simple Research Framework

Use this once and save the output. It will give you a repeatable process for evaluating any project going forward.

Prompt
Create a simple 10-point checklist I can use every time I research a new crypto project before investing. Focus on fundamentals, tokenomics, team, community and risk assessment.

How to Get the Best Results

These prompts work best with ChatGPT-4 or Claude. Always verify the output with your own research. AI gives you a starting point and not a final answer.

Replace the text in brackets with the actual coin name or concept you are researching. The more specific you are the better the output will be.
