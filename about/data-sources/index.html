Data Sources

Where Our Information Comes From

Reliable analysis begins with reliable information.

CoinGyaan is committed to transparency in how we gather, evaluate and reference the information used throughout our publication. Our articles, market intelligence and educational resources are developed using reputable public sources whenever possible.

Publishing our data sources allows readers to better understand the foundation of our research and evaluate our conclusions with greater confidence.

Whenever practical, we encourage readers to consult original sources alongside our analysis.

Our Approach to Data

Digital asset markets produce information from many different sources, each providing only part of the overall picture.

Rather than relying on a single platform or data provider, CoinGyaan evaluates information from multiple independent sources to build a broader understanding of market conditions.

Whenever possible, we compare information across different datasets before incorporating it into our research.

This approach helps improve accuracy, reduce bias and provide additional context for readers.

Primary Sources

Whenever available, we prioritize primary sources over secondary reporting.

These may include:

  • Official company announcements
  • Project documentation
  • Regulatory filings
  • Government publications
  • Official protocol documentation
  • Public blockchain records
  • Exchange announcements
  • Financial statements
  • Public investor presentations

Primary sources provide the most direct representation of events and reduce the risk of information being misinterpreted through repeated reporting.

Blockchain Data

Blockchain networks provide publicly accessible transaction data that supports many forms of market research.

Depending on the topic being analyzed, CoinGyaan may review publicly available blockchain information, including:

  • Transaction activity
  • Wallet activity
  • Token supply
  • Smart contract interactions
  • Network participation
  • Onchain transfers
  • Block production statistics

Blockchain data helps provide objective information that complements broader market analysis.

Blockchain Infrastructure

Some of our research relies on publicly accessible blockchain data retrieved through infrastructure providers and blockchain networks.

These services help us access onchain information such as transactions, smart contract activity and network statistics.

CoinGyaan may periodically update the infrastructure providers used as our platform evolves.

Market Data

CoinGyaan analyzes market conditions using data from multiple established digital asset market data providers and exchanges.

Depending on the topic being covered, our research may reference publicly available information from services including:

  • Binance Spot and Binance Futures
  • OKX
  • Bybit
  • Hyperliquid
  • CoinGecko
  • CoinPaprika
  • CoinLore
  • DefiLlama, including stablecoin market data
  • Alternative.me

These sources provide information such as:

  • Asset prices
  • Trading volume
  • Open interest
  • Funding rates
  • Stablecoin supply
  • Exchange reserves
  • Market capitalization
  • Liquidity indicators

Market conditions change continuously and values referenced in our articles reflect the information available at the time of publication.

Regulatory and Government Information

Regulatory developments frequently influence digital asset markets.

When covering legal or policy related topics, we reference information published by official organizations whenever possible.

These may include:

  • Government agencies
  • Financial regulators
  • Central banks
  • Securities regulators
  • Legislative bodies
  • Court filings
  • Public consultation documents

Using official documentation helps improve accuracy when reporting regulatory developments.

Research and Industry Publications

CoinGyaan may reference research produced by reputable organizations when it provides valuable context for readers.

These may include:

  • Academic institutions
  • Independent research firms
  • Financial institutions
  • Blockchain analytics companies
  • Industry associations
  • Economic research organizations

External research is used to supplement our own analysis rather than replace independent editorial judgment.

Attribution and Citations

Whenever practical, CoinGyaan attributes information to its original source.

Articles may include references, quotations or links to official documentation that allow readers to verify important claims independently.

We believe proper attribution strengthens transparency and supports informed decision making.

Information We Treat With Caution

Not all information circulating within digital asset markets meets the standards required for publication.

We exercise additional caution when evaluating:

  • Anonymous claims
  • Unverified social media posts
  • Market rumors
  • Screenshots without supporting evidence
  • Speculative predictions
  • Leaked documents that cannot be independently verified

Information from these sources is not treated as confirmed unless supported by additional evidence.

Continuous Review

Markets evolve quickly and new data providers, research tools and blockchain networks continue to emerge.

CoinGyaan periodically reviews the sources used throughout our editorial process to ensure they remain reliable, transparent and relevant.

As our research capabilities expand, this page will be updated to reflect meaningful changes in the types of information we rely upon.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinGyaan use public blockchain data?

Yes. Depending on the subject, our research may incorporate publicly available blockchain information alongside market data and official documentation.

Does CoinGyaan rely on a single data provider?

No. We evaluate information from multiple reputable sources whenever possible to improve accuracy and provide broader context.

Does CoinGyaan verify information before publication?

Yes. We make reasonable efforts to verify information through primary sources and reputable public documentation whenever available.

Why does CoinGyaan publish its data sources?

Transparency helps readers understand where our information comes from and strengthens confidence in our research process.
