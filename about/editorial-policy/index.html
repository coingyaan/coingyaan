Editorial Policy

Our Commitment to Responsible Journalism

CoinGyaan is committed to publishing accurate, independent and transparent journalism that helps readers better understand digital asset markets and blockchain technology.

Every article, market analysis and educational resource published by CoinGyaan is guided by editorial standards designed to prioritize accuracy, context and accountability. Our objective is not to influence markets or promote specific assets, but to provide readers with reliable information that supports informed decision making.

Trust is earned through consistency. We believe responsible reporting begins with clear editorial principles and a commitment to maintaining those standards across everything we publish.

Editorial Independence

CoinGyaan maintains complete editorial independence.

Editorial decisions are based solely on newsworthiness, research value and relevance to our readers. Commercial relationships, advertising partnerships or sponsorships do not influence our reporting, analysis or editorial opinions.

We clearly distinguish between editorial content, sponsored content and advertising whenever applicable. Sponsored or promotional material is identified to help readers understand the nature of the content they are viewing.

Our editorial team retains full authority over the topics we cover, the conclusions we publish and the research we present.

Accuracy and Verification

Accuracy is fundamental to our editorial process.

Before publication, our team makes every reasonable effort to verify facts using reputable and publicly available sources. When reporting on market developments, regulatory actions, company announcements or blockchain activity, we seek to confirm information through primary sources whenever possible.

These may include:

  • Official company announcements.
  • Regulatory filings.
  • Government publications.
  • Blockchain explorers.
  • Official protocol documentation.
  • Exchange announcements.
  • Publicly available market data.
  • Research reports from established organizations.

No verification process can eliminate every possibility of error. If inaccuracies are identified after publication, we will correct them as promptly and transparently as possible.

Research and Analysis

Market analysis often involves interpreting data rather than reporting objective facts alone.

Our analysis is based on available information, market conditions and observable trends at the time of publication. Financial markets can change rapidly and future developments may differ from our expectations.

When presenting analysis, we aim to distinguish clearly between factual reporting, market observations and informed interpretation.

Readers should understand that analysis reflects current market conditions and should not be interpreted as certainty or guaranteed outcomes.

Editorial Standards

Every piece of content published by CoinGyaan is developed with the following principles in mind:

  • Accuracy before speed.
  • Evidence before opinion.
  • Context before conclusions.
  • Transparency in methodology.
  • Independence from commercial influence.
  • Clear and accessible language.
  • Continuous review and improvement.

These standards apply equally to news articles, market intelligence, educational resources and research publications.

Sources and Attribution

Credible journalism depends on responsible sourcing.

Whenever practical, CoinGyaan attributes information to its original source and encourages readers to consult primary documentation when available.

We may reference information from:

  • Public blockchain data.
  • Government agencies.
  • Regulatory authorities.
  • Official company communications.
  • Academic research.
  • Financial institutions.
  • Market data providers.
  • Industry reports.

Information obtained from anonymous or unverified sources is treated with caution and is only published when it has been independently verified or when publication clearly serves the public interest.

Corrections Policy

Despite careful review, errors may occasionally occur.

When factual inaccuracies are identified, we are committed to correcting them promptly. Corrections may include updates to factual information, clarifications or revisions that improve the accuracy of published content.

Significant corrections may be noted within the article to provide transparency regarding material changes.

Readers who believe an article contains an error are encouraged to contact us at hello@coingyaan.com.

Artificial Intelligence and Editorial Oversight

CoinGyaan may use artificial intelligence tools to support research, content planning and editorial workflows.

All published content is reviewed through our editorial process before publication. AI generated material is not published without human oversight and editorial review.

Editorial responsibility always remains with CoinGyaan.

Financial Information Disclaimer

CoinGyaan publishes informational and educational content only.

Nothing published on this website constitutes financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Readers should conduct their own research and consult qualified professionals before making financial decisions involving digital assets or other investments.

Past market performance does not guarantee future results.

Our Responsibility to Readers

Our responsibility extends beyond reporting daily market developments.

We aim to provide context that helps readers understand why events matter, how markets evolve and what factors may influence future developments.

Every article is written with the goal of helping readers become more informed participants in the digital asset ecosystem rather than encouraging speculation or short term decision making.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinGyaan provide financial advice?

No. CoinGyaan publishes educational and informational content. Nothing on this website should be interpreted as personalized financial or investment advice.

How does CoinGyaan verify information?

We verify information using reputable public sources whenever possible, including official announcements, regulatory documents, blockchain data and established market data providers.

Can articles be corrected after publication?

Yes. If an error is identified, we will review the issue and update the article when appropriate to maintain accuracy and transparency.

Does advertising influence editorial decisions?

No. Editorial decisions are made independently and are not influenced by advertisers, sponsors or commercial partners.
