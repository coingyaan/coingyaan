Frequently Asked Questions

How can I report an error in an article?

Please email hello@coingyaan.com with the article URL, the information you believe is incorrect and any supporting evidence. We review all legitimate correction requests.

Does CoinGyaan accept partnership inquiries?

Yes. We welcome discussions with organizations whose products or services align with our publication and audience. Editorial independence is maintained at all times.

Can I request coverage of a project or topic?

Yes. While we cannot guarantee coverage, we welcome suggestions that may be relevant to our readers and editorial focus.

How quickly does CoinGyaan respond?

Response times vary depending on the nature of the inquiry and the volume of messages received. We make every effort to respond to legitimate inquiries as promptly as possible.
