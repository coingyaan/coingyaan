Cookie Policy

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how CoinGyaan uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

Cookies help us improve website functionality, understand how visitors interact with our content and provide a better browsing experience.

By continuing to use CoinGyaan, you acknowledge that cookies may be used as described in this policy, subject to your browser settings and applicable laws.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser when you visit a website.

They help websites remember information such as your preferences, browser session and how you interact with pages over time.

Cookies cannot directly access files stored on your device or install software.

Why We Use Cookies

CoinGyaan may use cookies to:

  • Keep the website functioning properly
  • Remember user preferences
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand visitor behavior
  • Measure audience engagement
  • Support security and fraud prevention
  • Deliver and measure advertising where applicable

Cookies help us continually improve the experience we provide to our readers.

Types of Cookies We May Use

Essential Cookies

Essential cookies help the website operate correctly.

These cookies may be used to support:

  • Basic website functionality
  • Security features
  • Page navigation
  • Session management

Without these cookies, certain parts of the website may not function as intended.

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use CoinGyaan.

This information may include:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Navigation patterns
  • Device categories
  • Traffic sources

Analytics data is used to improve content quality and overall user experience.

Advertising Cookies

Where advertising is displayed, advertising partners may use cookies to:

  • Deliver relevant advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Limit repetitive advertisements
  • Improve campaign reporting

These cookies may be placed by trusted third party advertising providers.

Preference Cookies

Preference cookies remember choices you make while using the website, such as language settings or other user preferences where applicable.

These cookies help personalize your browsing experience.

Third Party Cookies

Some services integrated into CoinGyaan may place their own cookies.

Examples may include:

  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising partners
  • Embedded media providers
  • Social media platforms

These third parties operate under their own privacy and cookie policies.

CoinGyaan does not control cookies placed by third party services.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies
  • Delete cookies
  • Block cookies
  • Limit third party cookies
  • Clear browsing data

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some areas of the website.
