About the Ethereum intelligence view

This page brings the CoinGyaan signals for Ethereum into one place. The outlook estimates the most probable direction over the next 24 hours from live market data. The metrics below it show the current price, the perpetual funding rate on Ethereum, open interest on Ethereum perpetuals, the market wide sentiment reading and momentum. Funding and open interest here are specific to Ethereum, while sentiment is a single market wide index shared across assets.

Read the outlook and the metrics together. A direction with strong agreement across funding, momentum and trend is more reliable than one where the signals conflict. Everything here is analysis to support your own research.
