Our Story

CoinGyaan started in 2017 as a crypto content channel on YouTube. I was focused on explaining blockchain basics and covering the latest crypto news and helping people understand this rapidly evolving space. Back then most crypto content was either too technical for beginners or too shallow to be genuinely useful. My goal was to bridge that gap.

I have been through two complete market cycles since then. The 2017 to 2018 bull run and crash. The 2020 to 2021 DeFi summer and NFT boom. The bear markets in between. The rise of airdrops and layer two networks and real world asset tokenization and institutional Bitcoin adoption. Every cycle teaches you something the previous one did not.

Over the years I covered everything from what is blockchain to DeFi yield strategies to memecoin mania and airdrop farming and the rise of infrastructure projects. I also built on several chains, participated in hackathons and launched tools including the Wallet Reputation Analyzer and a Fear and Greed Divergence Signal tool.

Why I Built This

After years of creating content I realized something important. The crypto community needs better tools not just more content. There is enough noise already. Hundreds of Twitter accounts posting charts. Thousands of YouTube videos explaining the same concepts. What people actually need are simple and useful tools that help them make sense of the market in real time.

Most sentiment tools are either paywalled or buried inside dashboards that take ten minutes to understand. I wanted to build something that gives you a clear answer in seconds. Bullish or bearish or neutral. What the Fear and Greed Index is saying right now. Whether it is altcoin season or Bitcoin season. All in one place and all free.

That is why CoinGyaan shifted from creator to builder. Every tool on this platform is something I personally wanted and could not find elsewhere for free.

What CoinGyaan Offers

Right now CoinGyaan offers a free crypto sentiment checker that analyzes RSI, price momentum, volume and the Fear and Greed Index for Bitcoin and 30 plus major altcoins. We also have a live Fear and Greed Index page, an Altcoin Season Index, Stablecoin Dominance tracker and a Today's Market Pulse summary that synthesizes everything into one verdict.

We recently launched the Airdrop Rescue Tool, a smart contract powered tool that helps users rescue eligible airdrop tokens stuck in compromised wallets before sweeper bots can act. This is one of the most specific and useful tools we have built and it came directly from seeing this problem in our Telegram community repeatedly.

We also publish free crypto guides covering Bitcoin, DeFi, technical analysis, airdrop strategies and AI tools. All guides are written based on genuine experience and research and all are free to read with no login required.

Our Philosophy

We believe the best tools are simple and free and genuinely helpful. We are not here to sell you a course or push some token. We are building things we wish existed when we started in crypto.

Everything on CoinGyaan is educational and informational. We do not provide financial advice. We give you the data and tools to make your own informed decisions. Our full disclaimer and editorial policy explain exactly how we create and review content.
