About CoinGyaan

CoinGyaan is a free crypto market intelligence platform built to help traders, investors, and researchers understand overall market sentiment using data-driven indicators.

The platform focuses on simplifying complex market emotions — such as fear, greed, bullishness, and bearishness — into clear, easy-to-understand signals that support informed decision-making.

CoinGyaan analyzes publicly available market data, sentiment indicators, and behavioral signals to reflect how the crypto market is feeling at any given time, including Bitcoin and major altcoins.

CoinGyaan does not provide financial or investment advice. All information published on this site is intended strictly for educational and informational purposes.

Our mission is to make crypto market data more accessible, transparent, and useful — whether you are new to crypto or an experienced market participant.

By offering simple tools like the Crypto Sentiment Checker and Fear & Greed Index, CoinGyaan aims to help users better understand market psychology without hype or speculation.
