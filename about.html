About CoinGyaan

From crypto content creator to tool builder.

Our Story

CoinGyaan started in 2017 as a crypto content channel on YouTube. Back then we were focused on explaining blockchain basics and covering the latest crypto news and helping people understand this rapidly evolving space.

Over the years we have covered everything from what is blockchain to DeFi summer and the NFT boom and memecoin mania and airdrop farming and the rise of infrastructure projects.

We have seen multiple bull runs and bear markets and everything in between.

The Shift

After years of creating content we realized something important. The crypto community needs better tools not just more content.

There is enough noise already. What people actually need are simple and useful tools that help them make sense of the market.

That is why we are shifting from creator to builder. CoinGyaan is now a platform where we build practical crypto tools that solve real problems.

What We Are Building

Right now CoinGyaan offers free market sentiment tools that help traders understand overall market psychology. Our Crypto Sentiment Checker and Fear and Greed Index analyze publicly available data to give you clear signals about whether the market is feeling bullish or bearish or neutral.

We focus on simplifying complex market emotions into easy to understand indicators. No hype and no speculation. Just data driven insights that support better decision making.

But this is just the beginning. We are planning to add more features and tools in the coming weeks.

Our goal is to make crypto market data more accessible and transparent and actually useful.

Our Philosophy

We believe the best tools are simple and free and genuinely helpful. We are not here to sell you a course or push some token.

We are building things we wish existed when we started in crypto.

Everything on CoinGyaan is educational and informational. We do not provide financial advice. We just give you the data and tools to make your own informed decisions.
