About CoinGyaan

CoinGyaan is a free crypto market intelligence platform designed to help traders, investors, and researchers understand overall market sentiment using data-driven indicators.

Our primary focus is on simplifying complex market emotions such as fear, greed, bullishness, and bearishness into easy-to-understand signals that can support better decision-making.

CoinGyaan combines public market data, sentiment signals, and behavioral indicators to provide insights into how the crypto market is feeling at any given time.

We do not provide investment advice. CoinGyaan is built purely for educational and informational purposes.

Our mission is to make crypto market data accessible, transparent, and useful for everyone — from beginners to experienced traders.
