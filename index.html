Crypto Intelligence FAQ

CoinGyaan converts live market signals into simple probability insights. Always free. No login required.

Crypto Probability Intelligence is CoinGyaan's approach to converting raw market signals into a single weighted score. It uses RSI, Fear and Greed, stablecoin dominance, BTC dominance and price momentum. The score shows how aligned current conditions are with historical bullish or bearish patterns. It does not predict future prices. It measures signal alignment so you can form your own view.

CoinGyaan calculates the BTC Market Outlook using multiple live crypto market signals rather than a single indicator. Bitcoin sentiment today, Fear and Greed Index values, RSI, BTC dominance, liquidity conditions and recent market momentum are combined into a weighted score. The score does not predict exact future prices. It highlights whether current signals lean bullish, bearish or neutral.

Bitcoin sentiment reflects how traders and investors currently feel about the market. Positive Bitcoin sentiment usually appears when confidence, liquidity and buying activity increase. Negative sentiment often appears during fear, uncertainty or stronger selling pressure. CoinGyaan tracks Bitcoin sentiment today using multiple market signals to help users understand overall crypto market sentiment.

The Fear and Greed Index on CoinGyaan updates regularly using live market data and sentiment indicators. It helps users understand whether current market conditions show fear, caution or optimism. Tracking the Fear and Greed Index today alongside Bitcoin sentiment and BTC market signals can provide additional context for market research.

Probability signals help users understand market conditions but they are not future price predictions. CoinGyaan uses crypto market signals such as Bitcoin sentiment, market momentum, volatility and liquidity data to estimate current conditions. These signals should be used as educational insights and combined with personal research rather than treated as guaranteed outcomes.
