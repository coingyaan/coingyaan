CoinGyaan converts live market signals into simple probability insights. Always free. No login required.

The BTC Market Outlook is CoinGyaan's directional prediction engine. It answers one question: what is the probability BTC moves higher over the next 24 hours? It uses five Binance-derived signals — momentum across three timeframes, RSI and RSI slope, funding rate, open interest direction and volatility. Fear and Greed, BTC Dominance and Stablecoin Dominance are shown separately as market context but have zero influence on this score.

The BTC Market Outlook score is calculated from five signals using Binance data only. Momentum across 1-day, 3-day and 7-day timeframes carries 40% of the weight. RSI and its slope carry 25%. Funding rate carries 15%. Open interest direction carries 10%. Volatility carries 10%. The result is an upside probability between 0% and 100% showing the strength of the directional signal for the next 24 hours.

The Contrarian Insight card shows the Fear and Greed Index as an educational signal only. Historically, extreme fear has created long-term accumulation opportunities while extreme greed has often preceded volatility. This card is completely separate from the BTC Market Outlook score and has no influence on the prediction engine.

The Fear and Greed Index on CoinGyaan updates regularly and is displayed in the Contrarian Insight section. It is informational context only and does not affect the BTC Market Outlook directional score. The prediction engine uses only Binance-derived momentum, RSI, funding rate, open interest and volatility data.

The BTC Market Outlook uses live derivatives and price data to estimate 24-hour directional probability. These are educational tools based on historical signal patterns, not guaranteed predictions. Strong alignment across momentum, RSI and funding rate historically improves directional reliability. Always combine these signals with your own research.
