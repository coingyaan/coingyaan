How Crypto Market Sentiment Works

Crypto markets are driven by emotions as much as data. Prices often react to fear, excitement, confidence and uncertainty. Understanding sentiment helps you see how traders and investors are feeling right now.

Bullish sentiment reflects optimism and confidence. Bearish sentiment signals caution or fear. Neutral sentiment usually means the market is waiting for a clear direction before making a move.

CoinGyaan combines four signals into a clear view of market mood: ETF flows, price context, Fear and Greed Index and RSI. Each signal tells a different part of the story.

What Is the Crypto Fear and Greed Index

The Crypto Fear and Greed Index measures market emotions on a scale from zero to one hundred. It shows whether fear or greed is dominating the market at any given moment.

Extreme fear can appear during downturns when confidence is low. Extreme greed often shows up when prices rise quickly and excitement peaks. Neither extreme lasts forever.

CoinGyaan displays this index alongside sentiment analysis so you can understand market emotions at a glance without digging through multiple sources.

Using Sentiment Responsibly

Market sentiment is a guide not a prediction. It helps you understand crowd behavior but does not guarantee any outcome. Always combine sentiment insights with your own research and risk management.

CoinGyaan is built for education and awareness not financial advice. The signals we show are data points to help you think, not instructions on what to buy or sell.
