    How Crypto Market Sentiment Works

    Crypto market sentiment reflects the overall mood of traders and investors. CoinGyaan combines news signals, price context, and market behavior to indicate bullish, bearish, or neutral conditions.

    What Is the Crypto Fear and Greed Index?

    The Fear & Greed Index measures emotions driving the crypto market. Extreme fear may signal caution, while extreme greed can indicate overheating.
