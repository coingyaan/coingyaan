    How Crypto Market Sentiment Works?

    Crypto markets are driven by emotions as much as data.

    Prices often react to fear excitement confidence and uncertainty.

    Understanding sentiment helps you see how traders and investors are feeling.

    Bullish sentiment reflects optimism and confidence.

    Bearish sentiment signals caution or fear.

    Neutral sentiment usually means the market is waiting for direction.

    CoinGyaan simplifies this by combining sentiment signals into a clear and easy to understand view of market mood.

    What Is the Crypto Fear and Greed Index?

    The Crypto Fear and Greed Index measures market emotions on a scale from zero to one hundred.

    It shows whether fear or greed is dominating the market.

    Extreme fear can appear during downturns when confidence is low.

    Extreme greed often shows up when prices rise quickly and excitement peaks.

    CoinGyaan displays this index alongside sentiment analysis so you can understand market emotions at a glance.

    Using Sentiment Responsibly

    Market sentiment is a guide not a prediction.

    It helps you understand crowd behavior but does not guarantee outcomes.

    Always combine sentiment insights with your own research and risk management.
