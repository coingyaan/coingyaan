Compliance and Regional Standards

CoinGyaan serves a global audience with a strong presence in the United States and Europe. All advertising placements comply with applicable regional regulations including the EU Markets in Crypto Assets regulation (MiCA) and US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines on digital advertising. Sponsored content and paid placements are always clearly labeled as Sponsored or Promoted so users can distinguish between editorial content and advertising. We do not accept advertising that makes unverified financial claims or promises returns on investment in any form. For advertisers targeting European audiences CoinGyaan operates in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). We do not share personally identifiable user data with advertisers. Any audience insights provided to partners are aggregated and anonymised with no individual user data included.
