Recover Airdrops From Compromised Wallets

A compromised wallet is one where a drainer has access. Any tokens that arrive can be swept before you can react. Airdrop allocations and vested rewards are especially vulnerable because claim events are predictable and publicly visible on-chain.

CoinGyaan Airdrop Rescue handles the recovery transaction so your tokens reach a safe address before a drainer can act. Select your project, enter your credentials and safe wallet address. CoinGyaan does the rest.

Why Vested Token Recovery Requires Timing

Vested tokens unlock on a schedule. Drainers know this too. Recovery works by executing the claim and transfer before the drainer can respond. CoinGyaan tracks supported project vesting schedules and executes at the right moment.
