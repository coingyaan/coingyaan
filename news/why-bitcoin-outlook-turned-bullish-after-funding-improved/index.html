Why Bitcoin Turned Bullish After Funding Improved

Monesh Kumar·July 19, 2026·5 min read

Bitcoin's short term outlook shifted from neutral to moderately bullish this week, and the clearest driver was not price. It was the derivatives market. Perpetual funding moved back to positive and open interest firmed, two signals that together suggest traders are willing to pay to hold long exposure again.

Key takeaways

  • Perpetual funding turned positive, meaning longs are paying shorts to hold their positions.
  • Open interest firmed alongside the move, which points to fresh capital rather than a short squeeze.
  • The shift is a 24 hour read on positioning, not a long term price forecast.

What funding actually tells you

Funding is the periodic payment exchanged between long and short holders of a perpetual contract. When funding is positive, longs pay shorts, which signals that traders are willing to pay a premium to stay long. A move from negative or flat funding back to positive is often an early tell that sentiment in the leveraged market is turning constructive.

On its own, positive funding is not bullish. Very high funding can mark a crowded, fragile long that unwinds violently. What matters is the combination. Modestly positive funding rising off a low base, with open interest building rather than spiking, is the healthier version of the signal.

Why open interest confirms it

Open interest is the total value of outstanding derivative positions. Rising open interest into a rising or steady price suggests new money is entering the market and taking directional risk. That is different from a short squeeze, where price rises while open interest falls as shorts are forced to close. This week's firming open interest alongside improving funding is the constructive pattern, not the fragile one.

What this means for the next 24 hours

Read together, the signals lean modestly bullish over a short horizon. That is a statement about positioning and probability, not a guarantee. The same derivatives that support a move can amplify a reversal if funding overheats.
